MULTI-DOMAIN RULES
1. HR verifies employment/onboarding records; IT owns email/account access.
2. Facilities owns physical power and room infrastructure; IT owns projectors/displays/lecture PCs/AV.
3. Finance owns the student financial ledger; HR does not resolve student tuition.
4. If two independent issues are present, split the work and route each part.
5. If the issue type is missing, ask a concise clarification.

CONFIDENCE AND HANDOFF
High confidence + supporting retrieval -> answer with source and section.
Medium confidence -> answer cautiously or ask a targeted clarification.
Low confidence or missing evidence -> do not fabricate; clarify or hand off to a human.
